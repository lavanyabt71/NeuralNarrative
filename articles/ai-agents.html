AI agents: more than a chatbot.

A chatbot mainly responds. An AI agent can work through a sequence of steps, use tools, inspect results, and adjust its approach toward a goal.

PLANACTLEARN

From response to workflow

Imagine asking for a research brief. A basic assistant might suggest an outline. An agent-style system could break the task into steps, search approved sources, organize findings, and prepare a draft for review.

Tools make the difference

Agents may be connected to search, code execution, calendars, or business software. These tools let them do more than generate text, but they also introduce permissions, security, and reliability concerns.

Autonomy needs boundaries

Good agent design defines what the system can access, which actions require approval, how errors are handled, and when a human must take over. For consequential actions — such as sending money or changing important records — explicit human confirmation is essential.

THE KEY IDEA

An agent is best understood as a model inside a controlled loop of planning, tool use, and feedback — not as an independent human-like mind.
